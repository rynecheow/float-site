Getting an account

Float is invite-only. Ask your household's treasurer to add your email, then sign in with the code Float emails you.

Can't sign in

Check the inbox of the email you typed, including spam. The code is 8 digits and works for 10 minutes; tap Send code again for a new one.

A receipt was read wrongly

Open the expense and fix any field. Nothing is saved until you confirm, and you can turn AI reading off in Settings and type receipts in yourself.

Delete your account

Settings, then Account, then Delete account. The privacy policy says what goes and what stays.

Contact

Open a support request. We reply within two working days.
