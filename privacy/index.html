Float

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Float is a shared ledger for one invited household. This page says what Float keeps, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

What Float keeps

  • Your email address and the name your household uses for you, to sign you in and show who paid.
  • What you record: expenses, splits, payments, notes, trips, card names and their last four digits, statements and investments.
  • Receipt photos and PDFs you add, kept with the expense they belong to. Receipts left unfinished are deleted on a schedule.
  • A place on an expense, only when you pick one or tap "Where I am now". Float never tracks your location in the background.
  • Crash reports and screen timings from your device, to find and fix problems. They never contain amounts, names or merchants, and are kept for 90 days.

Float has no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores Float's database and files, in Singapore.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model makers it routes to (such as DeepSeek, Google and Anthropic) read a receipt or statement you send, only after you allow AI reading in the app. They return what they read; Float keeps the result.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and reminder emails.
  • Apple delivers notifications. Telegram delivers reminders, only if you link it.

Who can see your data

Your household's treasurer sees the household's shared records. Other members see the shared expenses they are part of. Your private pages under Me are yours alone.

Deleting your account

In Float, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Your sign-in, email and private data are deleted. Shared expenses stay so that other people's balances don't change, and show you as "Former member". If the treasurer deletes their account, the whole household's data is deleted. Backups that still hold deleted data roll off within 90 days.

Children

Float is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the app links to the current version.
